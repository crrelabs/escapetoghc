Welcome to Paradise

Nestled in the heart of the Berry Islands, Great Harbour Cay is one of the Bahamas' best-kept secrets. With miles of pristine white sandy beaches, crystal-clear turquoise waters, and a warm, welcoming community, this island offers a rare escape from the modern world. Whether you're seeking adventure or pure relaxation, GHC delivers an authentic Bahamian experience you won't find anywhere else.

🏖️

Pristine Beaches

Miles of powdery white sand and turquoise waters — Sugar Beach, Shark Creek, and more.

World-Class Fishing

Island Adventures
