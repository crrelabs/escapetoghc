Where to Eat

Island dining at its finest

The Beach Club

Affectionately known as "Da Beach Club" — the heartbeat of Great Harbour Cay dining. Located right on the beach across from the airport. Famous for hamburgers, conch fritters, fish sandwiches, and the iconic Angie Special. Enjoy an ice-cold Kalik beer or Rum Punch with ocean views.

Tue–Sun: Breakfast 8–11am, Lunch 11am–3:30pm

Coolie Mae's

Perched on a Bullocks Harbour hill with sunset views. Old-world charm with homemade food — famous for Guava Duff dessert, specialty breads, and bean & dumpling soup every Thursday. Full bar, 60 seats, fresh seafood cooked any way you like.

Call ahead for orders — this local favorite gets busy!

Read More →

Driftwood

A beloved island spot serving up fresh Bahamian cuisine with a laid-back, beachside vibe. Known for fresh seafood, cocktails, and a welcoming atmosphere that brings locals and visitors together. A must-visit dining experience on Great Harbour Cay.

Check locally for hours

CarriEarl's / The Carriearl Roost

Located at the Soul Fly Lodge, north of the Beach Villas on Great Harbour Beach. A full restaurant run by a Bahamian Chef with indoor, outdoor, and al fresco dining. Full bar and cocktail lounge.

By reservation only. Thu–Sat: 7–9pm. Sun Brunch: 10am–1pm
