Buy Yourself a "GHC Louis"

On your Escape to Great Harbour Cay, you may get the itch to go shopping for souvenirs. There is a gift shop at the Marina named "Happy People's Rentals & Gift Shop" that will surely satisfy your craving for merch. There you will find a plethora of knick-knacks and gifts to take back home with you to cheer you up from the "GHC Blues."

And who needs a Louis Vuitton when you can get yourself a handmade straw beach bag? The GHC Louis' are made personally by the owner of the store. Her name is Ruth, and if you call ahead with a few days notice, Ruth will make you a custom bag ready to be picked up when you arrive on the island.

Give them a call at +1 242-367-8117 to get your GHC Louis!
