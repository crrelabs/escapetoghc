Green Flash Cottage

Luxury beachfront living on Great Harbour Cay

True Beachfront Luxury

Green Flash Cottage is a spacious 3-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom beachfront home sleeping up to 8 guests. With two levels of wrap-around decks, you'll enjoy panoramic ocean views from every angle — and there are no roads or barriers between you and the sand.

The chef's kitchen and open concept living spaces create a seamless indoor-outdoor flow perfect for island living. The primary bedroom features a king bed, en-suite bath, and ocean view deck. The second bedroom has a queen bed with private bath, and the third has two twins with a full bath. A loft with two additional twins provides extra sleeping space.

Enjoy daily towel service, daily housekeeping, high-speed WiFi, air conditioning, and a washer/dryer — all the comforts of home in paradise.
