Villa's Edge Beach Villa

Your beachfront home on Great Harbour Cay

The Perfect Island Getaway

Villa's Edge is a charming beachfront villa just steps from the powdery white sands of Great Harbour Cay. Sleeping 6+ guests, it's ideal for families, couples, or small groups looking for an authentic Bahamian escape.

The villa features a fully equipped kitchen with bar top seating, a comfortable living room with sofa bed, and a spiral staircase leading to a cozy loft with twin beds. The master bedroom opens onto an outdoor terrace perfect for morning coffee with ocean breezes.

Just a 5-minute walk from the Beach Club and near Coolie Mae's, Hammerheads, and CarriEarl's — you'll never be far from great island dining.
